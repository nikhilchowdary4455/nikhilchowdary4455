The presentation

Trust comes from three things

Explainability

Lets people see why a model produced an answer.

Validation

Tests the model on data it has never seen and checks that its reasoning matches its answers.

Performance metrics

Turn that testing into measurable evidence, such as accuracy, hallucination rate, fairness, robustness and faithfulness.

Real-world examples

Amazon's AI hiring tool

A resume-screening model learned bias against women from historical, mostly male resumes. The problem was only found after specialists studied its results, which shows why explainability and fairness testing matter.

ChatGPT and "strawberry"

The model said there are two "r" letters in "strawberry" (there are three) and often stood by the wrong answer. Models read text as tokens rather than letters, and a confident wrong answer with no visible reasoning is hard to trust.
